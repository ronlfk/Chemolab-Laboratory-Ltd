Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Fon_Dev (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with our mobile games and applications (collectively, the “Application” or “Service”). We have designed our Application with privacy in mind: it does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, and it does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

Summary

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not require accounts, registration, or sign-in.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not share, sell, or transmit user data to any third party.
  • All gameplay data stays locally on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

The Application does not request, collect, or transmit any of the following: name, email address, phone number, postal address, payment information, precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, microphone input, camera input, advertising identifiers, device identifiers used for tracking, IP address, browsing history, or any behavioral analytics.

Locally Stored Data

In order for the Application to function (for example, to remember your progress, points, scores, settings, or preferences), the Application may store data locally on your device using the operating system’s standard storage mechanisms (such as SharedPreferences or local files). This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us or any third party, and is removed when you uninstall the Application or clear its data from system settings.

No Third-Party SDKs

The Application does not integrate any third-party software development kits (SDKs), analytics frameworks, crash reporting services, advertising networks, social login providers, or marketing tools. No external libraries collect or transmit user data from the Application.

No Advertising

The Application does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising identifiers (such as GAID/IDFA) for any purpose.

Children’s Privacy

Our Application is suitable for general audiences, including children. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction). Our practices comply with the principles of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the GDPR-K provisions for children.

Permissions

The Application may request standard system permissions strictly necessary for app functionality (for example, the permission to display notifications). These permissions are used solely on-device for the stated purpose and do not result in any data being transmitted to us or to third parties. You can revoke any permission at any time through your device’s system settings.

Security

Because the Application does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no user data to be exposed in transit or on our servers. Any data the Application stores remains entirely on your device under the protection of your device’s operating system.

Your Rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA grant users rights regarding their personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or port that data. Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not hold any information about you that could be accessed, corrected, deleted, or transferred. You can remove all locally stored data at any time by clearing the Application’s data or uninstalling it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of the Application after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
