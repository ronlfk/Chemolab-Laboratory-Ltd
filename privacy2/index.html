Privacy Policy – Color Switch Memory

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Color Switch Memory (the “Game”), published by FON_DEV.

Color Switch Memory is designed to be played without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

Bundle ID: com.ColorSwitchMemor

2. Information We Collect

Color Switch Memory does not collect, store, process, transmit, or share personal information.

In particular, the Game does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details.
  • Account registration information.
  • Precise or approximate location information.
  • Photos, videos, audio recordings, or other user-generated content.
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers.
  • IP addresses.
  • Usage, analytics, diagnostics, or performance information.
  • Payment or financial information.
  • Contacts, messages, or files stored on the user's device.
  • Health, biometric, or other sensitive personal information.

3. No User Accounts

The Game does not require or support the creation of user accounts. Users are not required to submit personal information to download, access, or play the Game.

4. No Advertising or Tracking

The Game does not display third-party advertisements, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not track users for advertising, marketing, profiling, or attribution purposes.

5. No Third-Party SDKs

The Game does not integrate or use third-party software development kits for advertising, analytics, tracking, crash reporting, marketing attribution, social media integration, push notifications, or data collection.

6. Local Game Data

Any game progress, scores, settings, or preferences saved by the Game are stored locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to FON_DEV or any third party.

Users may remove locally stored game data by clearing the Game's data through their device settings or by uninstalling the Game.

7. Children's Privacy

Color Switch Memory does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Because the Game does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information belonging to children under the age of 13 or under the applicable age of digital consent.

8. Data Sharing and Retention

FON_DEV does not sell, rent, trade, disclose, or otherwise share users' personal information because the Game does not collect such information. FON_DEV does not retain personal information through Color Switch Memory.

9. Security

Although the Game does not collect personal information, reasonable measures are taken to maintain the integrity and security of the Game.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, including when changes are made to the Game, applicable laws, or platform requirements. Any updated version will include a revised "Last updated" date.
