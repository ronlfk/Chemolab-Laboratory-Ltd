Chemolab Laboratory Ltd and/or Eran Matitiahu ("Company", "we", "us") operates the Glow Up 75 mobile application ("App"). 2. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account details (name, email address) when registering via email, Google, or Apple Sign-In

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version) for app functionality and notifications
  • Usage data (features used, challenge progress) stored in your account

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Improve and maintain the App
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) – Authentication and data storage

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these providers.
